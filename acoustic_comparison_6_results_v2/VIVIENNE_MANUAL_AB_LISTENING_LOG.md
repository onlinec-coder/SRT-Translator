## Listening method

**Pass 1 — natural dynamics**
- Listen to Original, then Speech, then Original again.
- Do not normalize each cue independently.
- Note which version preserves the intended performance and natural dynamic contrast.

**Pass 2 — loudness-matched**
- Match playback loudness only to compare timbre, breath, articulation, and phrase onset/ending.
- Do not interpret loudness matching as proof that the source dynamics are equivalent.
